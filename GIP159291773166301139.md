GIP 13: StableHex -- Tiered Coverage Incentive Program
======================================================

**Executive Summary**

This proposal adopts StableHex incentives to aggressively reward:

· Deployment of new coverage in under served areas

· Long-term reliability of deployed base stations

· Prevention of coverage decay

  

It introduces:

· **Three reward tiers (6×, 4×, 2× multipliers)**

· **Fine-grade RRR calculation with consideration of Local Satellites number**

· **Delayed bonus rewards with a 30-day performance holdback**

· **Severe penalties for downtime**

**1\. Program Objectives**

This program directly targets:

**A. No Coverage**

Strong, front-loaded incentives for first-mover deployment in remote hexes.

**B. Coverage Went Away**

Delayed rewards and downtime penalties ensure operators maintain uptime beyond initial deployment.

**3\. Reward Structure**

Each eligible base station earns rewards as follows:

**Base Reward (1×)**

· Paid daily.

· Distributed normally.

**Bonus Reward (Multiplier − 1×)**

Held in custody for **30 days**.

Example:

· 6× Tier → 1× paid daily, 5× held.

· 4× Tier → 1× paid daily, 3× held.

· 2× Tier → 1× paid daily, 1× held.

At the end of 30 days, performance is evaluated.

**Performance Evaluation**

Base station up-time determines how much of the held bonus is released. Fine-grade RRR is calculated to capture up-time more precisely.

**4\. StableHex Selection Criteria**

Foundation will deploy StableHexes based on paid-customer needs and also considering following criteria:

**Tier 1 (6×)**

· A new base station is highly wanted in remote/hard-to-reach area

**Tier 2 (4×)**

· A new base station is critical to improve network coverage

**Tier 3 (2×)**

· A new base station is important to improve network coverage

  

The first active base station in the StableHex will take the awarding position (StableHex Miner).

If more than one base station in a StableHex:

· Current StableHex Miner will prevail if it can maintain RRR ≥ 99.5% at the 30-day checkpoint.

· If not, the best RRR (at least 98% ) miner that has been active for at least 30-day will become the StableHex Miner
